# Phase 1 Deliverables (Nov 2025 - Apr 2027)

---

## Overview

The AISESA AI Working Group is launching with **four priority deliverables** over our initial 12-18 months. These are designed to:
- Build foundational understanding of AI and governance across our membership
- Create practical frameworks that organizations can actually use
- Share knowledge and build community
- Lay groundwork for long-term African AI governance leadership

---

## 1. Member Skills & Needs Survey

### What It Is
A comprehensive assessment of AI literacy, current usage, governance awareness, and capacity-building priorities across AISESA member organizations.

### Why It Matters
Before we can develop useful governance frameworks, we need to understand:
- What AI tools and applications are already in use?
- What governance challenges are organizations facing?
- What training and support would be most valuable?
- How can we prioritize our work to address real needs?

**This survey ensures our frameworks are grounded in African realities, not assumptions.**

### How to Engage
- **Participate**: Complete the survey when launched (Q1 2026)
- **Share**: Encourage colleagues and partner organizations to respond
- **Contribute**: Suggest questions or topics the survey should address

### Timeline
- **Q1 2026**: Survey design and pilot testing
- **Q2 2026**: Launch and data collection
- **Q2 2026**: Analysis and reporting
- **Q3 2026**: Findings integrated into frameworks

## 2. TRUST Matrix v0.1

### What It Is
A practical framework for assessing AI systems across five governance dimensions:
- **T**ransparency: Can we understand how the AI works and makes decisions?
- **R**eliability: Does it perform consistently and accurately?
- **U**nderstandability: Can diverse stakeholders engage with it meaningfully?
- **S**afety: Does it protect people, infrastructure, and environment?
- **T**rustability: Do we have confidence in the overall system?

Each dimension has **maturity levels** (1-5) to help organizations assess where they are and what governance measures are appropriate.

### Why It Matters
Not all AI systems require the same level of governance. A chatbot answering customer questions needs different oversight than an AI managing national grid stability.

**The TRUST Matrix helps organizations:**
- Assess their AI systems objectively
- Identify governance gaps and priorities
- Match governance requirements to actual risk
- Communicate about AI in structured, comparable ways

### How to Engage
- **Share use cases**: Help us test the framework with real examples
- **Provide feedback**: Review draft definitions and assessment criteria
- **Pilot the tool**: Try applying TRUST Matrix to your AI systems
- **Join the working group**: Contribute to framework development

### Timeline
- **Q1 2026**: Draft dimension definitions and levels
- **Q2 2026**: Use case mapping and validation
- **Q3 2026**: Public release of v0.1
- **Ongoing**: Refinement based on real-world use

## 3. AI Ethics Principles v0.1

### What It Is
A set of **African-centered ethical commitments** for AI in the energy sector, addressing:

1. **Just Transitions & Equity**: Ensuring AI benefits marginalized communities and addresses historical inequities
2. **Transparency & Accountability**: Clear decision-making and defined responsibilities
3. **Data Sovereignty & Privacy**: African control over African data
4. **Safety & Reliability**: Robust protections for people and infrastructure
5. **Capacity Building**: Investment in African AI expertise
6. **Environmental Sustainability**: Alignment with climate and renewable energy goals
7. **Cultural Context**: Respect for diverse African values and knowledge systems

### Why It Matters
Many existing AI ethics frameworks are developed in the Global North for Global North contexts. They may not adequately address:
- Energy poverty and access challenges
- Data sovereignty and digital colonialism concerns
- Capacity constraints and technology transfer needs
- Indigenous knowledge and cultural diversity
- Just transition priorities in resource-dependent economies

**African-owned principles ensure our values shape AI development, not vice versa.**

### How to Engage
- **Contribute perspectives**: What ethical issues matter most in your context?
- **Review drafts**: Provide feedback on principle statements
- **Share challenges**: Help us ground principles in real governance dilemmas
- **Apply in practice**: Test principles in your AI projects and share lessons

### Timeline
- **Q1 2026**: Draft principle statements
- **Q2 2026**: Stakeholder consultation
- **Q3 2026**: Public release of v0.1
- **Ongoing**: Case studies and implementation guidance

## 4. Inaugural Webinar

### What It Is
A public knowledge-sharing event to:
- Launch the TRUST Matrix and AI Ethics Principles
- Share findings from the member survey
- Present use cases from across African energy sector
- Facilitate dialogue on AI governance priorities
- Build community and momentum

### Why It Matters
Governance isn't just about frameworks on paper—it's about **shared understanding** and **collective action**. The webinar:
- Makes our work accessible to broader audiences
- Creates space for peer learning and networking
- Demonstrates African thought leadership on AI governance
- Invites feedback and new collaborators

### How to Engage
- **Attend**: Participate in the webinar (date TBD, likely Q3-Q4 2026)
- **Present**: Share your organization's AI use case or governance approach
- **Spread the word**: Help us reach relevant stakeholders
- **Provide input**: Suggest topics, speakers, or format ideas

### Timeline
- **Q2 2026**: Planning and speaker recruitment
- **Q3-Q4 2026**: Event execution
- **Q4 2026**: Documentation and follow-up activities

## Beyond Phase 1: What's Next?

These four deliverables lay the foundation for ongoing work:

**Phase 2 Possibilities** (2027 and beyond):
- Detailed implementation playbooks for specific use cases
- Regional workshops and training programs
- Certification or self-assessment tools
- Policy briefs for African energy regulators
- Integration with continental AI strategies (African Union, regional economic communities)
- Expanded use case library and knowledge commons

**Your input shapes our roadmap.** Join the conversation and help us prioritize.
