# AIG4JETA Governance Toolkit
[Home](index.md) • [TRUST Matrix](trust-matrix.md) • [AI Ethics Principles](ai-ethics-principles.md) • [Use Case Gallery](use-case-gallery.md)

Welcome to the **AI Governance Toolkit for Just Energy Transitions in Africa (AIG4JETA)**, developed by the AISESA AI Working Group.

This space provides a **public, GitBook-friendly view** of our core governance artifacts, use cases, and processes. It is meant for:

- Policy-makers and regulators  
- Utilities and energy companies  
- Civil society and community organizations  
- Researchers and practitioners

---

## What You’ll Find Here

### 1. Governance Frameworks

- **TRUST Matrix v0.1** – a practical framework to assess AI use cases in the energy sector across five dimensions:  
  Transparency, Risk & Reliability, Understanding (Context), Safety & Security, and Trustworthiness (Ethics).  
- **AI Ethics Principles v0.1** – African-centered principles to guide responsible AI in just energy transitions.

These are the **core reference documents** for evaluating and governing AI projects in the energy space.

---

### 2. Surveys & Capacity Building

The toolkit includes a **Member Skills & Needs Survey** and related instruments to:

- Understand the current AI and governance literacy of stakeholders,  
- Identify capacity gaps, and  
- Prioritize training and support activities.

---

### 3. Use Case Library

The Use Case Library documents **realistic and emerging AI applications** in the African energy sector, using a common template that:

- Describes context and stakeholders,  
- Maps each case to the TRUST Matrix and Ethics Principles,  
- Captures governance and community engagement practices, and  
- Shares lessons learned for scaling or replication.

Example:

- **UC-001 – AI-based Load Forecasting for Rural Mini-Grids in East Africa**

See the **Use Case Gallery** for the growing list of documented use cases.

---

### 4. How to Use This Toolkit

If you are:

- **Exploring AI in energy:**  
  Start with the **TRUST Matrix** and **AI Ethics Principles** to understand key questions you should be asking.

- **Designing or evaluating a project:**  
  Use the **Use Case Template** and TRUST Matrix to document and assess your initiative.

- **Building capacity in your institution:**  
  Adapt the **Member Skills & Needs Survey** to map capabilities and design training.
