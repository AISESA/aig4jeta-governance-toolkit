## About AIG4JETA

### What is AIG4JETA?
**AIG4JETA** stands for "AI Governance for Just Energy Transitions in Africa." It's an initiative of the AISESA AI Working Group to develop practical, African-owned frameworks and tools for responsible AI adoption in the energy sector.

### Who is this for?
- **Energy sector professionals** using or considering AI technologies
- **Policy makers and regulators** developing AI governance approaches
- **Researchers** studying AI, energy, or governance
- **Civil society organizations** advocating for just energy transitions
- **Technology providers** working in African energy markets

### Why focus on Africa specifically?
African energy systems face unique challenges and opportunities that global AI governance frameworks may not adequately address:
- Energy access gaps and last-mile challenges
- Diverse regulatory environments
- Data sovereignty and digital colonialism concerns
- Just transition priorities in resource-dependent economies
- Need to build local AI capacity

African-owned frameworks ensure our values and priorities shape AI development, not vice versa.

## About the Frameworks

### What is the TRUST Matrix?
The TRUST Matrix is a practical tool for assessing AI systems across five dimensions:
- **T**ransparency
- **R**eliability
- **U**nderstandability
- **S**afety
- **T**rustability

Each dimension has maturity levels (1-5) to help organizations understand where they are and what governance measures are appropriate for different types of AI systems.

See the [full TRUST Matrix explainer](trust-matrix-explainer.md).

### How is this different from other AI ethics frameworks?
Many existing frameworks (IEEE, EU AI Act, etc.) are developed in the Global North. While valuable, they may not adequately address African contexts. Our frameworks:
- Are **African-owned** and grounded in African realities
- Focus on **energy sector** specifics
- Prioritize **just transitions** and equity
- Emphasize **implementation** over abstract principles
- Address **data sovereignty** and capacity building

We align with relevant global standards where appropriate but center African priorities.

### Are these frameworks mandatory?
No. These are **voluntary guidance** developed collaboratively by the AISESA working group. They're meant to help organizations implement responsible AI, not to impose top-down regulations.

However, we hope regulatory bodies may reference or adopt elements of these frameworks over time.

## Getting Involved

### I'm not technical. Can I still contribute?
**Absolutely!** We need diverse perspectives, especially from:
- Policy and governance experts
- Community and civil society voices
- Energy sector operators and practitioners
- People affected by energy AI systems

Contributing doesn't require coding skills. See our [Contributing Guide](contributing-guide.md).

### How much time does it take to participate?
It's flexible:
- **Minimal**: Fill out our survey (15 min), occasional feedback on drafts
- **Light**: Monthly working group meetings (1-2 hours), reviewing documents
- **Active**: Lead a deliverable, develop use cases, present at webinars

### Do I need to use GitHub?
GitHub helps us collaborate and track changes, but it's not required. You can:
- Provide feedback via email
- Participate in meetings and webinars
- Share use cases through the working group chair

We're happy to help you learn GitHub if you're interested!

### Is this only for AISESA members?
The **working group** is primarily AISESA members, but we welcome:
- **Feedback and suggestions** from anyone
- **Use case submissions** from across the African energy sector
- **External expertise** on specific topics

All outputs are **open and publicly accessible**.

## About the Work

### What's the timeline?
**Phase 1** (12-18 months, Nov 2025 - Apr 2027) focuses on:
1. Member skills & needs survey
2. TRUST Matrix v0.1
3. AI Ethics Principles v0.1
4. Inaugural webinar

See the [Phase 1 Deliverables](phase-1-deliverables.md) for details.

### How are decisions made?
- **Consensus preferred** through open discussion
- **Working group vote** when consensus isn't reached
- **Documented** in our decision log for transparency

See our [governance processes](https://github.com/USERNAME/aig4jeta-governance-toolkit/blob/main/process/decision-log.md).

### How will you measure success?
**Quantitatively**:
- Number of organizations using the frameworks
- Survey participation and geographic diversity
- Use cases documented

**Qualitatively**:
- Frameworks actually improve AI governance in practice
- African voices recognized in global AI policy discussions
- Growing community of practice

### What happens after Phase 1?
Phase 2 will build on Phase 1 foundations, potentially including:
- Implementation playbooks and training programs
- Regional workshops
- Policy briefs for regulators
- Integration with continental AI strategies
- Expanded use case library and knowledge commons

The community will help shape Phase 2 priorities.

### Can I use these frameworks in my organization?
**Yes!** Once published, you're free to:
- Apply the frameworks in your AI projects
- Adapt them to your specific context
- Share them with partners and stakeholders

We ask that you:
- Provide attribution to AISESA AI Working Group
- Share feedback on what works and what doesn't
- Consider contributing your experience back to the community

### Can I translate these materials?
**Yes, please!** We encourage translation into African languages. Please:
1. Let us know via GitHub or email
2. Follow any formatting/quality guidelines we provide
3. Share translations back with the community

### Where's the code?
This is primarily a **frameworks and documentation** project, not a software project. However:
- All materials are version-controlled on GitHub
- We may develop assessment tools or calculators in the future
- Use case documentation may include technical implementation details
