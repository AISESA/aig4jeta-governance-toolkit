# Welcome to AIG4JETA

**AI Governance for Just Energy Transitions in Africa**

***

## What is AIG4JETA?

AIG4JETA is an initiative of the **AISESA AI Working Group** to develop practical, African-owned frameworks and tools for responsible AI adoption in the energy sector.

We believe that Africa's energy transitions—from expanding renewable energy to improving grid reliability to extending energy access—can be accelerated by AI technologies. But only if those technologies are deployed **transparently**, **safely**, **equitably**, and in ways that **build African capacity** rather than extracting value.

**Our approach**:

* 🌍 **African-owned**: Grounded in African contexts, values, and priorities
* 🛠️ **Implementation-focused**: Practical tools, not abstract policy
* 🤝 **Collaborative**: Built in the open with diverse stakeholders
* 📈 **Evidence-based**: Informed by real use cases and member needs
