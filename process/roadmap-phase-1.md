# Phase 1 Roadmap (Nov 2025 - Apr 2027)

**Duration**: 12-18 months
**Focus**: Foundation-building and initial deliverables

---

## Overview

Phase 1 establishes the core frameworks, evidence base, and community needed for long-term AI governance work.

**Key Principles**:
- Start with member needs and real use cases
- Build iteratively with frequent feedback
- Maintain transparency and open collaboration
- Deliver practical, usable outputs

## Key Dependencies & Risks

### Dependencies
- AISESA membership engagement and survey participation
- Volunteer capacity from working group members
- Use case submissions from organizations
- External stakeholder input on ethics principles

### Risks & Mitigation
| Risk | Impact | Likelihood | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|------------|
| Low survey response rate | Medium | Medium | Multi-channel outreach; incentives; simplified instrument |
| Limited use case submissions | Medium | Medium | Direct outreach; anonymization options; showcase value |
| Volunteer bandwidth constraints | High | High | Clear scope; distributed responsibilities; prioritize ruthlessly |
| Frameworks too abstract or complex | Medium | Medium | Ground in real use cases; plain language; iterative testing |

---

## Success Metrics

**Quantitative**:
- ≥50 survey responses from diverse organizations and countries
- ≥10 use cases documented and mapped to TRUST Matrix
- ≥100 webinar participants
- Frameworks adopted by ≥3 organizations in pilot implementations

**Qualitative**:
- Positive feedback on framework usability and relevance
- Evidence of frameworks influencing AI governance decisions
- Growing working group and contributor community
- Recognition in African AI policy discussions

## Governance & Decision-Making

**Working Group Structure**:
- **Chair**: Overall coordination and strategic direction
- **Deliverable Leads**: Responsible for specific outputs (survey, TRUST, ethics, webinar)
- **Contributors**: All working group members participate in reviews and provide input

**Decision Process**:
- Consensus for most decisions
- Vote (simple majority) when consensus not achievable
- See [`decision-log.md`](decision-log.md) for record
