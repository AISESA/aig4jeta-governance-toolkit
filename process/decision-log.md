## Decision Process

**Who Decides?**
- **Working group co-chairs**: Operational and procedural decisions
- **Full working group**: Major governance artifact changes, strategic direction
- **AISESA leadership**: Decisions affecting broader organization (budget, partnerships, etc.)

**How?**
- **Consensus preferred**: Seek agreement through discussion
- **Vote if needed**: Simple majority for most decisions; 2/3 majority for governance artifacts
- **Documented**: All decisions logged here with rationale
