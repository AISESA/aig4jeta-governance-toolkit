# AISESA JET-AI Governance Principles (Draft)

**Purpose:** To define the core ethical values guiding the development and deployment of AI for Just Energy Transitions.

---

## 1. Principle of Justice & Equity
* **Commitment:** AI systems must actively reduce energy poverty and not exacerbate existing inequalities.
* **Practice:** All deployments must include an "Equity Impact Statement" assessing benefits for marginalized communities.

## 2. Principle of Transparency
* **Commitment:** No "black box" algorithms in critical grid infrastructure.
* **Practice:** Stakeholders must have access to understandable explanations of how AI models make decisions (e.g., load shedding schedules).

## 3. Principle of Safety & Reliability
* **Commitment:** Grid stability and human safety take precedence over optimization or profit.
* **Practice:** Mandatory "Human-in-the-Loop" protocols for any high-stakes automated control systems.

## 4. Principle of Data Sovereignty
* **Commitment:** African energy data should be governed by African institutions.
* **Practice:** Data storage and processing should prioritize local infrastructure where feasible; data sharing requires explicit consent.

## 5. Principle of Collaborative Governance
* **Commitment:** Governance is a shared responsibility, not a top-down mandate.
* **Practice:** Continuous feedback loops with member utilities, regulators, and civil society groups.
