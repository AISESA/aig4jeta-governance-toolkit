## 2. Working Group Mandate & Scope

### Shared Focus
**Theme**: AI Governance for Just Energy Transitions in Africa (AIG4JETA)

**Core Principles**:
- **African-owned and context-specific**: Grounded in African realities, not imported frameworks
- **Implementation-focused**: Practical tools over abstract theory
- **Collaborative and open**: Transparent processes and shared outputs

### Key Objectives
1. Develop governance frameworks appropriate for African energy contexts
2. Build capacity for responsible AI adoption
3. Create practical tools and guidance
4. Share knowledge and best practices across AISESA membership

### Deliverable 2: TRUST Matrix v0.1
**Purpose**: Framework for assessing AI governance requirements across use cases

**TRUST Dimensions**:
- **T**ransparency
- **R**eliability
- **U**nderstandability
- **S**afety
- **T**rustability

**Key Activities**:
- Define each dimension and assessment criteria
- Establish maturity levels (1-5 scale)
- Map use cases to TRUST profiles
- Validate with working group and broader membership

**Target Timeline**: Draft Q1 2026, validation Q2 2026, publication Q3 2026
