# TRUST Matrix v0.1 – AI Risk Assessment Framework for Energy

**Version:** 0.1 (Draft)  
**Scope:** Evaluation of AI models used in grid management and energy distribution within African just energy transitions.

---

## 1. T – Transparency

**Objective:** Ensure stakeholders can understand what data was used and how the model makes decisions.

- **Data Lineage:** Is the training data source clearly documented?  
  - [ ] Yes (Source: ____________________)  
  - [ ] No

- **Explainability:** Can the model's decision-making process be interpreted by non-technical stakeholders?  
  - [ ] High (Rule-based / Linear)  
  - [ ] Medium (Feature importance available)  
  - [ ] Low (Black box neural net)

---

## 2. R – Risk & Reliability

**Objective:** Understand operational risk and reliability requirements.

- **Failure Impact:** What is the worst-case scenario if the model fails?  
  - [ ] Minor (Data logging error)  
  - [ ] Moderate (Temporary local outage)  
  - [ ] Critical (Grid instability / Safety hazard)

- **Uptime Requirement:** Is 99.9% availability required?  
  - [ ] Yes  
  - [ ] No

---

## 3. U – Understanding (Context)

**Objective:** Ensure contextual alignment with African realities and local energy systems.

- **Local Relevance:** Was the model trained on data relevant to the specific African region of deployment?  
  - [ ] Yes, local data used  
  - [ ] No, pre-trained on global/western datasets only

- **Stakeholder Buy-in:** Have local community leaders or affected stakeholders been consulted?  
  - [ ] Yes  
  - [ ] No

---

## 4. S – Safety & Security

**Objective:** Protect energy systems and communities from harm arising from AI use.

- **Adversarial Robustness:** Has the model been tested against cyber-attacks (e.g., data poisoning, model hijacking)?  
  - [ ] Tested & verified  
  - [ ] Testing in progress  
  - [ ] Not tested

- **Human-in-the-Loop:** Is there a human operator required to approve high-stakes decisions?  
  - [ ] Yes  
  - [ ] No

---

## 5. T – Trustworthiness (Ethics)

**Objective:** Align deployment with energy justice and ethical AI principles.

- **Bias Auditing:** Has the model been checked for bias against specific demographics, regions, or customer classes (e.g., rural vs. urban, low-income households)?  
  - [ ] Audit passed  
  - [ ] Audit pending  
  - [ ] Not applicable

- **Energy Justice:** Does this deployment support equitable energy access and avoid disproportionate burdens on vulnerable communities?  
  - [ ] Yes  
  - [ ] No

---

*Reference: Adapted for the AISESA Just Energy Transition Initiative (AIG4JETA).*
