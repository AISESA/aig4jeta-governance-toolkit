# TRUST Matrix v0.1

**T**ransparency · **R**eliability · **U**nderstandability · **S**afety · **T**rustability

---

## Overview

The TRUST Matrix is a multi-dimensional framework for assessing and categorizing AI systems based on their governance requirements, risk profiles, and operational contexts in the energy sector.

**Purpose**: Provide a practical tool for:
- Assessing AI governance readiness across different use cases
- Matching governance requirements to system characteristics
- Guiding implementation priorities for AI ethics principles
- Facilitating cross-organizational comparison and learning
