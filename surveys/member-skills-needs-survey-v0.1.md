# Member Skills & Needs Survey v0.1

**AISESA AI Working Group: Baseline Assessment**

---

## Survey Objectives

**Primary Goals**:
1. Establish baseline understanding of AI literacy and governance awareness across AISESA membership
2. Identify priority training and capacity-building needs
3. Map current AI tool usage and use cases in member organizations
4. Inform development of TRUST Matrix and ethics principles
5. Guide working group priorities for Phase 1 and beyond

**Target Completion**: Q1 2026
**Expected Response Time**: 15-20 minutes

---

## Target Respondents

**Primary Audience**: AISESA member organization representatives, including:
- Energy sector professionals
- Policy makers and regulators
- Researchers and academics
- Technology providers and developers
- Civil society and community organizations
