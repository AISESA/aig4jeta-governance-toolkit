# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The AISESA AI Working Group is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free environment for all contributors, regardless of:
- Age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression
- Level of experience, education, socioeconomic status
- Nationality, personal appearance, race, religion
- Sexual identity and orientation
- Any other characteristic protected by law

We value diversity and recognize that effective AI governance requires perspectives from across Africa's diverse contexts, sectors, and communities.

---

## Our Standards

### Expected Behavior

**Be Respectful**
- Value different viewpoints and experiences
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully
- Focus on what is best for the community and working group goals
- Show empathy toward other community members

**Be Inclusive**
- Use welcoming and inclusive language
- Be mindful of language barriers and cultural differences
- Ensure materials are accessible (plain language, multiple formats when possible)
- Actively invite participation from underrepresented voices

**Be Collaborative**
- Work openly and transparently
- Share knowledge and credit others' contributions
- Seek consensus and compromise where appropriate
- Recognize that disagreement is normal and can be productive

**Be Professional**
- Stay on topic and goal-oriented in discussions
- Provide evidence and reasoning for claims
- Acknowledge uncertainty and limits of expertise
- Separate critique of ideas from critique of people

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:
- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Violent, threatening, or sexually explicit language or imagery
- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information (doxxing)
- Trolling, spamming, or sustained disruption
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All working group meetings and events
- GitHub repository interactions (issues, pull requests, discussions)
- Email communications and other working group channels
- Representing the working group in public forums

---

## Enforcement

### Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to:
- **Working Group Chair**: Lawrence Agbemabiese
- **AISESA Leadership**: [Contact details TBD]

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.

### Investigation

Reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly. The process includes:
1. Acknowledgment of report within 48 hours
2. Investigation and gathering of relevant information
3. Discussion with involved parties as appropriate
4. Decision on appropriate response

### Responses

Depending on the severity and context, responses may include:
- Private warning and request for behavior change
- Temporary suspension from working group participation
- Permanent removal from working group
- Reporting to relevant authorities (for serious violations)

### Appeals

Decisions can be appealed by contacting AISESA leadership.
